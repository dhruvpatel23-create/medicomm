GIVEN BELOW IS AN IMAGE OF ACTIVATION OF THE COAGULATION CASCADE, FOLLOWING CLOSED ENDOTHELIAL
INJURY. IDENTIFY THE FACTOR ‘L’ WHICH COMBINES WITH TISSUE FACTOR TO ACTIVATE FACTOR IX.

Options & Answer

  • FACTOR XII
  • FACTOR VII ✓ (Correct)
  • FACTOR VIII
  • VWF

Educational Rationale

THE FACTOR MARKED AS ‘L’ IS FACTOR VII, WHICH COMBINES WITH TISSUE FACTOR THAT IS PRODUCED FOLLOWING A CLOSE ENDOTHELIAL INJURY TO ACTIVATE FACTOR IX. AS THE IMAGE REPRESENTS THE IN VIVO COAGULATION CASCADE, THE TISSUE FACTOR IS THE MAJOR INITIATOR OF COAGULATION. IT IS BELIEVED THAT IN VIVO, FACTOR VIIA/TISSUE FACTOR COMPLEX IS THE MOST IMPORTANT ACTIVATOR OF FACTOR IX AND THAT FACTOR IXA/FACTOR VIIIA COMPLEX IS THE MOST IMPORTANT ACTIVATOR OF FACTOR X. TISSUE FACTOR IS EXPRESSED ON THE SURFACES OF THE SUBENDOTHELIAL CELLULAR COMPONENTS OF THE VESSEL WALL, SUCH AS SMOOTH MUSCLE CELLS AND FIBROBLASTS. THIS TISSUE FACTOR IS EXPOSED TO BLOOD WHEN THERE IS A VASCULAR DAMAGE. THIS ACTIVATES THE IN VIVO EXTRINSIC PATHWAY OF COAGULATION CASCADE, WHICH IS AS FOLLOWS: - TISSUE FACTOR BINDS WITH SERINE PROTEASE FACTOR VIIA - THIS COMPLEX THEN ACTIVATES FACTOR IX TO FACTOR IXA - FACTOR IXA THEN ACTIVATES FACTOR X TO FACTOR XA - FACTOR XA CAN BE FORMED THROUGH THE ACTIONS OF EITHER THE TISSUE FACTOR-FACTOR VIIA COMPLEX OR FACTOR IXA (WITH FACTOR VIIIA AS COFACTOR) 2014 - FACTOR XA THEN CONVERTS PROTHROMBIN TO THROMBIN, WHICH IS THE PIVOTAL PROTEASE OF THE COAGULATION CASCADE. - THE COFACTOR ESSENTIAL FOR THIS CONVERSION IS FACTOR VA. - THE THROMBIN THEN FORMED CONVERTS THE SOLUBLE PLASMA FIBRINOGEN TO AN INSOLUBLE FIBRIN MATRIX. - THROMBIN ALSO ACTIVATES FACTOR XIII, WHICH IS THE FIBRIN STABILIZING FACTOR, TO FACTOR XIIIA. THIS FACTOR XIIIA FORMS COVALENT CROSS-LINKS AND THEREBY STABILIZES THE FIBRIN CLOT. THE BELOW IMAGE SHOWS THE IN VIVO COAGULATION CASCADE.

Educational Rationale

THE GIVEN IMAGE SHOWS ASYMMETRIC SEPTAL HYPERTROPHY AND ALONG WITH THE SCENARIO, IT IS SUGGESTIVE OF SUDDEN CARDIAC DEATH DUE TO ARRHYTHMIA. PATIENTS WITH HYPERTROPHIC CARDIOMYOPATHY HAVE AN INCREASED RISK OF SUDDEN CARDIAC DEATH FROM VENTRICULAR TACHYARRHYTHMIAS. IN FACT, ARRHYTHMIAS ARE THE MOST IMPORTANT/MOST COMMON CAUSE OF DEATH IN HYPERTROPHIC CARDIOMYOPATHY. HYPERTROPHIC CARDIOMYOPATHY IS ONE OF THE MOST COMMON CAUSES OF SUDDEN, OTHERWISE UNEXPLAINED DEATH IN YOUNG ATHLETES. HCM IS CHARACTERIZED BY REDUCED STROKE VOLUME DUE TO IMPAIRED DIASTOLIC FILLING, WHICH IS A CONSEQUENCE OF THE MASSIVELY HYPERTROPHIC LEFT VENTRICLE. THERE IS A DYNAMIC OBSTRUCTION TO THE LEFT VENTRICULAR OUTFLOW AS THE ANTERIOR MITRAL LEAFLET MOVES TOWARDS THE VENTRICULAR SEPTUM DURING SYSTOLE. THIS COMPROMISED CARDIAC OUTPUT IN COMBINATION WITH A SECONDARY INCREASE IN PULMONARY VENOUS PRESSURE IS THE REASON FOR EXERTIONAL DYSPNEA AT TIMES OF INCREASED OXYGEN DEMAND. MAJOR CLINICAL PROBLEMS IN HCM ARE ATRIAL FIBRILLATION, STROKE, INTRACTABLE CARDIAC FAILURE, VENTRICULAR ARRHYTHMIAS AND ALSO SUDDEN DEATH. THERE IS A HARSH SYSTOLIC EJECTION MURMUR CAUSED BY THE VENTRICULAR OUTFLOW OBSTRUCTION. MUTATIONS CAUSING HYPERTROPHIC CARDIOMYOPATHY (HCM) ARE MOST COMMONLY SEEN IN THE GENES ENCODING β-MYOSIN HEAVY CHAIN (MHC/MYH7) OR MYOSIN-BINDING PROTEIN C (MYBP-C). HCM IS MOSTLY AN AUTOSOMAL DOMINANT DISORDER. THE MOST IMPORTANT HISTOLOGIC FEATURES OF HCM MYOCARDIUM ARE AS FOLLOWS: - MASSIVE MYOCYTE HYPERTROPHY PARTICULARLY OF TRANSVERSE MYOCYTE DIAMETERS - MYOFIBER DISARRAY INCLUDING THAT OF BUNDLES OF MYOCYTES, INDIVIDUAL MYOCYTES, AND CONTRACTILE ELEMENTS - INTERSTITIAL AND REPLACEMENT FIBROSIS 2030 MOST OF THE PATIENTS CAN BE TREATED WITH β BLOCKERS TO DECREASE HEART RATE AND CONTRACTILITY. IMPLANTABLE CARDIOVERTER-DEFIBRILLATORS ARE USED IN PATIENTS WITH INCREASED RISK OF VENTRICULAR ARRHYTHMIAS. SOME PATIENTS CAN ALSO BE TREATED BY SURGICAL EXCISION OF THE SEPTAL MUSCLE OR BY CAREFULLY CONTROLLED SEPTAL INFARCTION THROUGH A CATHETER-BASED INFUSION OF ALCOHOL. Educational Rationale

THE GROSS IMAGE OF THE LIVER SHOWS A NUTMEG APPEARANCE, SEEN IN HEART FAILURE. THE DARK AREAS REPRESENT NECROSIS, WHITE AREAS REPRESENT VIABLE LIVER TISSUE. NUTMEG LIVER REFERS TO THE MOTTLED APPEARANCE OF THE LIVER WHICH RESEMBLES THE CUT SECTION OF NUTMEG. THIS OCCURS AS A RESULT OF HEPATIC VENOUS CONGESTION FOLLOWING RIGHT HEART FAILURE. THE DARK CENTRILOBULAR ZONES REFLECT SINUSOIDAL CONGESTION. CONGESTION IS SEEN AROUND THE CENTRAL VEINS USUALLY FROM RIGHT-SIDED HEART FAILURE. THE PALE PERIPORTAL ZONES REFLECT NORMAL OR FATTY LIVER TISSUE. THE LIVER DERIVES ITS BLOOD SUPPLY FROM THE PORTAL VENOUS SYSTEM AND THE HEPATIC ARTERY. THE PORTAL TRIAD CONSTITUTES: - BRANCHES OF THE HEPATIC ARTERY - PORTAL VEIN - BILE DUCT 2045 THE BLOOD FROM THE PORTAL VEIN AND THE HEPATIC ARTERY FLOW THROUGH THE INTERVENING SINUSOIDS AND DRAINS INTO THE CENTRAL VEIN. THE CENTRAL VEINS FROM ADJACENT LOBULES FORM INTERLOBULAR VEINS, WHICH THEN UNITE AS HEPATIC VEINS AND FINALLY DRAIN INTO THE INFERIOR VENA CAVA. THE HEPATIC LOBULE IS DIVIDED INTO THREE ZONES: - ZONE 1 IS THE PERIPORTAL AREA - ZONE 2 IS THE INTERMEDIATE ZONE - ZONE 3 IS THE AREA CLOSEST TO THE CENTRAL VEIN RIGHT-SIDED CARDIAC FAILURE LEADS TO PASSIVE CONGESTION OF THE LIVER. LEFT-SIDED CARDIAC FAILURE LEADS TO HYPOPERFUSION AND HYPOXIA OF HEPATOCYTES AROUND CENTRAL VEINS. THE COMBINATION OF HYPOPERFUSION AND RETROGRADE CONGESTION CAUSES CENTRILOBULAR HEMORRHAGIC NECROSIS, LEADING TO THIS MOTTLED APPEARANCE OF THE LIVER, KNOWN AS NUTMEG LIVER. THE MICROSCOPIC IMAGE BELOW OF A NUTMEG LIVER SHOWS PERISINUSOIDAL FIBROSIS, HEPATIC VENULE DILATION, AND DILATION OF THE SINUSOIDS IN ZONE III.
